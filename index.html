Field guide
Expansion speed

Replay. The same bot count and seed give you the same coast and terrain. Click land once to start 30 seconds of expansion for everyone.

Economy. Every nation gets 35 coins when expansion ends. After that, nations split 10 coins per second by population share. Factories, ports and trade bring in extra coins.

Combat. Each tile settled during expansion adds four people. As soon as your borders meet, set a force in Orders and click the rival's land. Each nation continues expanding during the fight. A force that falls to half strength captures land at 35% speed. Send at least twice the defenders to take the whole nation at once.

Construction. Select a building in Build, then click an empty site in your territory. A captured building serves its new owner.

Diplomacy. Answer incoming offers directly on the map. Allies protect your shared border, trade pays both sides after expansion and a truce buys 20 seconds of peace. Rivals also negotiate with one another. Their decisions appear in Diplomacy. If all surviving rivals are your allies, you can claim an allied victory after expansion.

Military help. Choose an ally in Diplomacy to request an attack on a rival or reinforcements at home. An attacking ally needs a border with the rival and claims the land it takes. Defenders can travel across the island and stay for up to 20 seconds. When an ally asks you for troops, the request shows exactly how many you would send.

Map controls. Scroll to zoom. With the map focused, use arrow keys to inspect a tile and Enter to select it.
